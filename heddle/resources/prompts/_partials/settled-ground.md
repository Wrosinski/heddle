Settled ground: the runtime injects the already-adjudicated decision set —
the workspace decisions ledger and, verbatim from the Feature Spec, its
`## Decision Log` — as an Adjudicated Decision Ground or Disposition Rerun
Ledger block whenever one applies. Decisions listed there are settled
ground: do not re-raise, reword, or derive variants of them, and the
absence of enforcement for a documented accepted-risk assumption is not a
finding. A finding that contradicts settled ground is in scope only with
new evidence explicitly naming the decision it challenges; a rejected
recommendation is a recorded owner decision, not an open question —
rediscovering the design characteristic it covers is not new evidence.
